🔔 Bell Schedules

From the school's bell schedule sheet (2/5/2025). The Home dashboard switches to Mass or Half Day bells automatically on those days; pick Delayed Opening by hand on snow-delay mornings. Note: the sheet does not list lunch on Mass days; 12:00-12:45 is the gap between Periods 5 and 6.

🖨️ Print / Export PDF

Builds a clean lesson-plan packet with Objective, Activities, and Closure for every class. In the print window that opens, choose Save as PDF to make a PDF, or pick a printer to print the hard copy.

Tip: in your notes, start lines with Obj:, Act: and Close: and the export sorts them into the right boxes. Unlabeled notes print under Activities, and empty boxes print as blank lines you can handwrite on.

📂 Restore Backup

Merge adds this backup's content to what is already on this device: missing weeks are added, overlapping days are combined, and nothing here gets deleted. Use it to carry work between the tablet and a computer.

Replace everything wipes this device first and makes it exactly match the backup. Only use it on a new or reset device.
