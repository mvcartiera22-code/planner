Weekly Scoring Guide & How Grades Are Figured
A5 / 5 — ¡Excelente!Spanish only, active volunteer, positive team player. 100%
B4 / 5 — ¡Buen Trabajo!Good effort & attitude, basic greetings only, less volunteering, minor distraction. 80%
C3 / 5 — ¡Poco a Poco!English default, speaks only when called on, frequently off-task. 60%
D2 / 5 — ¡Cuidado!English almost exclusively, constant redirection needed, low engagement. 40%
F1 / 5 — ¡Ojo!Avoids Spanish entirely, negative or disruptive, unprepared. 20%
Tap a day box to cycle: A → B → C → D → F → Abs (absent) → blank.
Absences never lower a grade. An Abs day is left out of the average entirely — the week is scored only on the days the student was actually there. A student marked absent every class shows “Absent” instead of a grade.
The columns are this grade’s class days, with the real date for the week you are viewing. Set which weekdays each grade meets under Days & Calendar.
No-school days come from the Saint Paul School 2026–27 calendar, already loaded — a holiday or vacation greys the column out automatically and names the reason. Tap any day heading to mark a one-off closing yourself (snow day, assembly). Either way that day leaves every student’s average.
Early dismissal (12:30) days show an amber note but keep counting, since school is still in session. If Spanish did not meet that day, tap the heading to close it.
Weekly grade = the average of the classes that actually counted, so a one-class week is graded on that one class.
Bonus + initiated Spanish outside class (+0.5 to +1 per instance).   Needs Reflection Sheet lights up automatically at 3/5 (60%) or lower.   Notes — tap the notes box to type a note (great on the desktop) or write with the S Pen.
